About Us

School Location

Colong-Colong, Corcuera, Romblon, Philippines

Vision

We dream of Filipinos who passionately love their country and whose values and competencies enable them to realize their full potential and contribute meaningfully to building the nation.

Mission

  • Protect and promote the right of every Filipino to quality, equitable, culture-based, and complete basic education.
  • Provide a child-friendly, gender-sensitive, safe, and motivating school environment.
  • Work together with parents, teachers, and the community to help every learner succeed.

Academic Programs

Kindergarten Program

Play-based learning that builds early language, numeracy, social skills, and love for school.

Key Stage 1 (Grades 1–3)

Foundation in reading, writing, and arithmetic, taught in the learner’s mother tongue and Filipino.

Key Stage 2 (Grades 4–6)

Deeper learning in English, Science, Math, Araling Panlipunan, MAPEH, and EPP.

Learner Services

Enrollment Information

Requirements: PSA birth certificate, report card (SF9), and learner’s LRN. Enrollment forms are in Downloads.

Guidance and Counseling

Support for learners’ academic, social, and emotional needs, with referrals when needed.

Health and Nutrition Programs

Deworming, height and weight monitoring, and school-based feeding.

Child Protection Policy

Our school is a safe space. Report any form of abuse, bullying, or violence to the school head or the Child Protection Committee.
